## Servers

The project runs no VPN servers of its own. Your traffic goes only to the servers you added yourself: with a configuration, a link, a QR code or a subscription.

A server sees which addresses you reach through the tunnel and when, and it hands out the settings, the routing lists and the DNS itself. Add only a server you trust: your own or one run by someone you know.
